The Architecture

Why the SSU/SSE ↔ CRS Bridge?

By separating local on-ramps (SSU/SSE) from international settlement (CRS), we create a compliant, scalable, and lightning-fast remittance corridor.

Frequently Asked Questions

It is the core liquidity hub of the Web3 remittance ecosystem. It allows instant, low-cost swaps between local fiat-pegged SSU and the pegged CRS on the BASE chain.

The platform maintains a target 1 SSU ≈ 0.01 CRS peg (e.g., 100 SSU = 1 CRS). A flat 0.3% trading fee is charged by the liquidity pool to reward providers and maintain the system.

SSU acts as a clean local on-ramp. By using SSU, we separate local banking compliance from global settlement, making the entire process faster, cheaper, and more regulatory-friendly.

No KYC is needed for pure token-to-token swaps on the liquidity pool. KYC is only required at the local fiat on/off-ramp gateways when moving money between bank accounts and SSU.

Swaps are confirmed in seconds on the BASE chain. Once you approve the transaction in your wallet, the tokens are exchanged instantly.
