Regulatory Standards

Compliance & Trust

SSU HK Gateway is built on a foundation of regulatory integrity, ensuring every transaction meets Hong Kong's highest financial standards.

SFC-Compliant Framework

The platform architecture adheres to the Securities and Futures Commission's regulatory framework for virtual asset trading platforms (VATPs) in Hong Kong. All operational processes are designed to satisfy VATP licensing criteria.

AML and KYC Checks

Mandatory Know Your Customer (KYC) identity verification and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) screening are enforced for all users. Verification is powered by industry-standard identity document checks and sanctions screening.

Segregated Operational Processes

Client fiat funds are held in segregated accounts, strictly separated from operational capital. Token issuance and redemption workflows are independently audited to ensure full reserve backing at all times.

Transaction Monitoring

Continuous, real-time transaction monitoring detects and flags suspicious activity. All flagged transactions are reviewed by the compliance team in accordance with HKMA and SFC reporting obligations.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SSU HK Gateway.

What is SSU HK Gateway? +

SSU HK Gateway is an academic FinTech project developed at HKUST. It serves as the Hong Kong end of a cross-border Web3 financial ecosystem, enabling users to deposit HKD via FPS or bank transfer and receive SSU tokens in their Web3 wallet.

Is KYC verification mandatory? +

Yes. All users are required to complete KYC identity verification before making any deposits. This is a regulatory requirement under the SFC framework and Hong Kong's AML/CFT obligations. Verification typically takes 1–2 business days.

What is the HKD to SSU conversion rate? +

SSU tokens are issued at a 1:1 peg to HKD. For every 1 HKD deposited (net of applicable fees), 1 SSU token is issued to your wallet. The platform maintains full reserve backing to support this peg.

How long does a deposit take to process? +

FPS deposits are confirmed in near real-time (typically within seconds). Bank transfers settle on T+1 (next business day). eHKD settlements, once available, will enable atomic real-time settlement.

Is this platform regulated by the SFC? +

SSU HK Gateway is an academic prototype designed to operate within the SFC's regulatory framework for virtual asset trading platforms (VATPs). As a HKUST FinTech research project, it demonstrates compliance-by-design principles aligned with SFC licensing requirements.
